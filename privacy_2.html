Foodsaga Kitchen for iPhone

Privacy Policy

Effective 14 September 2026 · Kaan Tarakçıoğlu, Türkiye

The short version

  • Your kitchen — level, cash, ingredients and dishes — is saved on your phone. We keep no copy and run no server.
  • We run ads through Google AdMob, which uses device identifiers to choose and measure ads.
  • Purchases go through Apple. We never see your card, and we never sell anyone's data.

What the game stores on your phone

There are no accounts in this version and no sign-in. Everything the game remembers is written to your phone's own storage and stays there:

  • Your chef name, level, cash balance, tap multiplier and ad progress
  • The ingredients, dishes and restaurants you have collected
  • A record of items you have purchased, so the game can restore them

None of it is sent to us — we do not run a server, and there is no copy of your kitchen anywhere but your own device. Delete the app and it is gone.

Advertising

Foodsaga Kitchen shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. To choose which ad to show and to confirm you watched it, Google collects information such as an advertising identifier, your approximate location by country, device type, and how you interacted with the ad.

On iOS, the game can only use the advertising identifier if you allow it when your phone asks. If you decline, ads still appear — they are simply less relevant.

What Google does with that information is governed by its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Buying Remove Ads stops all ads. The optional "watch an ad for a free spin" cards stay available if you want them.

Purchases

All in-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles the payment and tells the game only whether the purchase succeeded. Your payment card, billing address and Apple Account details are never shared with us.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent anyone's personal information.
  • We do not run analytics or tracking SDKs beyond the advertising described above.
  • We do not ask for your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.

Children

Foodsaga Kitchen is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help you remove it.

Your choices

  • Turn off ad personalisation: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and switch Foodsaga Kitchen off.
  • Erase everything: delete the app, or use Reset in the game's Settings. Because it all lives on your phone, there is no copy for us to hold.
  • Ask us a question: write to the address below and we will answer.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version replaces this page and the effective date at the top is updated. Meaningful changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

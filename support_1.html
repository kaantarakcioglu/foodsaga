Support

Something broken, a question, or an idea for the game? Email foodsagakitchen@gmail.com and you'll get a reply.

Common questions

I bought something and it didn't arrive.
Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases. That asks Apple for everything you've bought and re-applies it. If it still doesn't show up, email us with the date of the purchase.

I bought Remove Ads but I still see ads.
Same fix — tap Restore Purchases in Settings. Remove Ads clears all forced ads; the optional "watch an ad for a free spin" cards stay by choice.

I got a new phone and my kitchen is gone.
This version saves your progress on the device itself, so it doesn't travel between phones. Purchases do — Restore Purchases brings those back. Cloud saves are coming in a future update.

How do I start over?
Settings → Reset. It warns you first, and it erases everything permanently.

The ad won't load.
Ads need a connection, and sometimes there just isn't one available to show. Try again in a minute.
